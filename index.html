About Me

A little about who I am

I'm Khaled Fadhel, a Data Analyst, developer, and problem-solver based in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. I graduated with honors in Information Technology from Saudi Electronic University (GPA 3.62), and I've spent my career sitting at the intersection of data, technology, and people.

My professional journey has taken me from managing and validating large datasets at Palm Studio, to supervising a team of eight and keeping operations running without a hitch, and freelancing on everything from IoT projects with Arduino to responsive web apps built in React. I like work that requires both technical depth and clear thinking. Whether that's analyzing academic papers, architecting a system, or debugging something no one else can figure out.

Feel free to reach out, I'm always open to conversations about collaboration and new ideas.
